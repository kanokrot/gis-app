I’m Kanokrot Sribuabun. I study Cybersecurity at Khon Kaen University and learn by building things and taking them apart.

I play CTFs and write up how I solved them, from picoMini forensics challenges to the network security part of WTCTT 2024. Writing a walkthrough forces me to explain every step, and that is when I find out what I actually understood.

Outside of CTFs I build for the web and for maps. The HouseBuy demo on this site is an ArcGIS experiment: a rotatable map with Thailand’s transport network, OSM places and NYC taxi fares, built with Angular.
